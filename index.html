The Promise

What we're building

Anchor launches with zero free tokens for the team. The contract is renounced the moment it's live — no one, including us, can mint more tokens, change the rules, or touch the liquidity.

We're anonymous by design. Not because we're hiding something — because trust shouldn't depend on a face. It should depend on math, on a contract anyone can read, and on a community that holds us accountable.

We will show up every single day. The team doesn't need to be famous. The token just needs to be un-rugable.
